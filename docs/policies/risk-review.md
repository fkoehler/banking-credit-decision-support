# Credit risk review policy

This is a fictional policy created solely for the repository demo.

## Capacity assessment

The reviewer compares requested financing, existing liabilities, sustainable
annual income and practice revenue. Late payments require clarification. A short
operating history requires stronger evidence of liquidity and market viability.

## Model use

The probability of default is one input to the review. Model version, input data,
retrieved policy passages and overrides must be recorded. The score cannot approve
or reject a case and must not be presented as a causal explanation.

## Escalation

Cases with incomplete documentation, contradictory data or material uncertainty
are returned for more information. A reviewer must not infer missing facts from a
generated narrative.
