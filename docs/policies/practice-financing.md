# Practice financing policy

This fictional policy applies to financing for physicians, dentists, pharmacists
and therapists. It is synthetic training material for a software demonstration.

## Required documents

Applicants provide the two most recent annual financial statements, a current
business assessment, evidence of professional authorization and a financing plan.
Practices younger than three years additionally provide quarterly liquidity plans.

## Equity

An equity contribution around twenty percent is considered a positive factor.
Lower equity does not automatically reject an application, but it requires a
documented review of collateral, cash flow and the reason for the lower amount.

## Human decision

Statistical scores and generated explanations are decision-support information.
A qualified reviewer remains accountable for the final decision and records the
rationale independently from the model output.
